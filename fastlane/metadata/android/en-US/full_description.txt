Swish is an independent Android video and music client maintained by ArsDevs.

- Video playback, quality selection, captions and background playback
- A dedicated music player, queues and lyrics
- Shorts, local history, subscriptions, playlists and downloads
- Local recommendation engines and Google Takeout import
- Optional account storage for learned recommendation profiles

Account profiles and enabled third-party features make network requests. Read
the privacy policy for details. Takeout does not provide YouTube's private Home
model. Swish is GPL-3.0 software and is not affiliated with YouTube or Google.
